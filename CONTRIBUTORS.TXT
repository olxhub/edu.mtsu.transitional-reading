Contributors
============

Course
======

The course content was developed by Timothy Nelson, Rebecca McIntyre,
Allison Brown, and Michele Abernathy of Middle Tennessee State University,
with review and feedback from the READ 1000 course team.

Ben Pierce and Piotr Mitros did substantial work adapting and revising this
course content for the platform, working with Anthropic's Claude models
(Fable and Opus), which drafted much of the revision under their direction
and review.

Textbook excerpts are adapted from The American Yawp
(https://www.americanyawp.com/), copyright the American Yawp contributors,
licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.

Platform
========

This module is built with lo-blocks, part of the Learning Observer project,
originally conceived of and created by Piotr Mitros. See the lo-blocks
repository (https://github.com/olxhub/lo-blocks/) for full platform
contributor history.
