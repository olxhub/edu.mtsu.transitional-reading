## The American Yawp, Chapter 11 — The Cotton Revolution

### Women and the Sphere of Woman

*Read carefully and use the highlighting tools to take notes.*

In the era of revivalism and reform, Americans understood the family and home as the hearthstones of civic virtue and moral influence. Women, the dominant understanding of gender claimed, were expected to be the guardians of virtue and the spiritual heads of the home. They were to be pious, pure, submissive, and domestic. Their responsibility was to pass these virtues on to their children.
