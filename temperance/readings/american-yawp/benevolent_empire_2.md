## American Yawp, Chapter 10 — Part 2

### The Benevolent Empire

*Read carefully and use the highlighting tools to take notes.*

Because of the Industrial Revolution, middle-class evangelicals had time and resources to devote to reform campaigns, where they exercised their sense of moral superiority over the working-class rabble and the privileged upper-class to try and create a respectable, hard-working middle-class culture. Middle-class women, in particular, played a leading role in reform activity…These women came together in order to deal with issues that primarily affected women and children, such as alcohol abuse, education reform, prostitution, gambling laws, and health reform.
